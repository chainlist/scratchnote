# Space stats

A panel docked beside the day, where a page docks, with:

- how many notes and pages the space holds, and how many days you wrote on;
- your streak, the days in a row you wrote, up to today;
- a heatmap of the last weeks, a square a day, brighter the more you wrote. A click on a square opens its day.

Open it with the chart button above the day, or **Show space stats** in the command center. It updates as you write.

## Settings

Settings > Community plugins > Space stats chooses how many weeks the heatmap shows: 12, 20 or 26.
